### HOW TO CANCEL (READ FIRST)

To cancel your subscription you **must** submit your request via the official Physique Academy Cancellation Form:

> **https://physiqueacademy.com/cancel**

This form is the **only valid** method of cancellation. WhatsApp messages, text messages, verbal requests, or emails to your coach do **not** count as a cancellation request and will not stop your subscription.

You will receive an automated email confirmation containing your reference number as soon as your request is logged.

## 1. MINIMUM COMMITMENT PERIOD

**1.1** All online coaching subscriptions with Physique Academy are subject to a **minimum commitment period of six (6) months** from the date your subscription begins.

**1.2** You acknowledge that you are entering into a six-month minimum term and that early cancellation does not remove your obligation to pay for the full minimum period.

**1.3** If you attempt to cancel before completing your six-month minimum period, you will remain liable for all payments due for the remainder of that minimum term. These charges will be collected in accordance with your payment schedule.

**1.4** No refunds will be issued for payments already collected during the minimum commitment period.

## 2. ROLLING MONTHLY SUBSCRIPTION (POST MINIMUM TERM)

**2.1** Upon completion of your six-month minimum term, your subscription will automatically continue on a **rolling monthly basis** at the same rate, billed on the same date each month.

**2.2** No further action is required from you to continue your subscription. Payments will continue to be collected until a valid cancellation request is received and processed in accordance with Section 3.

**2.3** You authorise Physique Academy and its payment processors to continue collecting payments on this rolling basis until your subscription is formally cancelled.

## 3. CANCELLATION PROCESS & NOTICE REQUIREMENTS

**3.1** Cancellation requests are **only accepted after completion of the minimum six-month period**. Any request received before this time will not be processed and does not relieve you of your payment obligations under Section 1.

**3.2** To cancel your subscription, you must submit a formal cancellation request via the Physique Academy Cancellation Form at **https://physiqueacademy.com/cancel**. Verbal requests, WhatsApp messages, or emails to your coach do not constitute a valid cancellation request.

**3.3** You must give a **minimum of one (1) full calendar month's notice** before your next scheduled payment date. Notice must be submitted on or before your payment date to take effect for that billing period.

**3.4** **Example:** If your payment date is the 10th of the month, you must submit your cancellation request on or before the 10th of the previous month. A request submitted on the 11th will not be processed until the following month's billing cycle.

**3.5** **At least one (1) final payment will be collected** following submission of your cancellation request, in accordance with the one-month notice requirement. You will not be entitled to a refund of this final payment.

**3.6** You will receive a written confirmation of your cancellation request via email, along with the date your final payment will be collected and the date your subscription will end. You should retain this confirmation for your records.

## 4. PAYMENT NOTICES

**4.1** Physique Academy will send you an automated email notification **seven (7) days before each scheduled payment**, confirming the amount due and the payment date. This notice will also include a direct link to the cancellation form should you wish to cancel.

**4.2** It is your responsibility to ensure that your email address on file is current and that payment notification emails are not filtered as spam. Physique Academy accepts no liability for missed payment notices where the notification has been sent to the email address you have provided.

**4.3** Failure to read or respond to a payment notification does not constitute grounds for a payment dispute or chargeback.

## 5. DISPUTES & CHARGEBACKS

**5.1** By signing this agreement, you acknowledge that all charges made to your payment method by Physique Academy are in accordance with the terms you have agreed to herein.

**5.2** You agree **not to initiate a chargeback or payment dispute** with your bank or card provider for any payment that has been collected in accordance with these terms. If you have a concern about a charge, you agree to contact Physique Academy directly in the first instance.

**5.3** In the event of a chargeback, Physique Academy reserves the right to submit this signed agreement, your payment history, email communication logs, and any other relevant evidence to the payment processor and/or card network in defence of the charge.

**5.4** Any fraudulent or unjustified chargebacks may be referred for debt recovery and may result in legal action.
