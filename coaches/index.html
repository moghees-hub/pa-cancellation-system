The Cancellation Policy and Your Role

A short, focused guide on what the signed policy is, what's expected from you, and how to chase clients who haven't signed yet.

Why this matters to you

You're the closest person to your client. If they ever cancel later and try to dispute a charge, the only thing standing between Physique Academy and a lost chargeback is the policy document they signed at onboarding. Your job is to make sure every one of your clients signs it, and to know the policy well enough to handle questions confidently.

The Policy in Plain English

Every new client signs a Cancellation & Billing Policy Agreement at onboarding. You don't need to read the full contract every time, but you must know the headline terms:

  • 6-month minimum commitment. Clients cannot cancel before completing 6 full months on the programme.
  • One final payment after cancellation. When a client cancels, one more monthly payment will still be collected. This is not refundable.
  • One month's notice. Cancellation must be requested at least 1 calendar month before the next payment date.
  • Form-only cancellation. The only valid way to cancel is via the official form at physiqueacademy.com/refunds-cancellations. WhatsApp messages, emails, or verbal requests directly to you do not count.

What Your Client Receives at Onboarding

Once a new client completes the onboarding questionnaire, they automatically receive an email with the cancellation policy to sign. Here's what they see, so you can recognise both if a client asks.

Your Responsibilities

  1. Know the policy So you can answer client questions confidently. The 4 headline terms above are enough for most conversations.
  2. Check the tracker dashboard weekly Open the Cancellation Policy Tracker, filter by your name, and look for any of your clients who haven't signed yet.
  3. Chase unsigned clients Use the templates further down this page. Polite, clear, with the signing link.
  4. Refer any cancellation requests to the official form If a client asks you directly to cancel them, do not action it. Send them the form link. No exceptions.

How to Use the Tracker

The Cancellation Policy Tracker shows every new active client and whether they've signed the policy. There's a coach filter at the top so you can narrow it to only your clients.

Step 1

Log in

Open pa-cancellation-tracker-production.up.railway.app and enter the team password. Ask Moghees or check the team Slack if you don't have it.

Step 2

Filter by your name

Use the coach dropdown above the table and pick yourself. The list now shows only your clients. Your selection is remembered, so next time you log in you'll already be filtered.

Step 3

Click the "Awaiting Signature" tab

This shows only your clients who haven't signed yet. Anyone here needs chasing.

Step 4

Hit "Send Reminder"

The dashboard fires an automated reminder email with a fresh signing link. You can send up to 3 reminders. After 3, the cap stops automated reminders and you should reach out personally via WhatsApp or chat.

Chase Message Templates

If a client needs more than a reminder email, send a personal message. Here are templates you can copy and paste. Keep them warm, short, and never apologetic about the policy itself.

First nudge - friendly

Best for clients in their first 2 weeks

Hey [Name], welcome to Physique Academy. Quick admin one for you: I noticed you haven't signed our Cancellation & Billing Policy yet. It's standard for everyone joining and only takes a minute. Could you have a look when you get a chance? Should be in your email inbox from us, but let me know if you can't find it.
Second nudge - clearer

If they still haven't signed after a few days

Hey [Name], just bumping this up. We need every client to sign the Cancellation & Billing Policy as part of onboarding, otherwise we can't continue your programme properly. It's quick. Let me know if you're having trouble finding the email and I'll get it resent to you.
Third nudge - firmer

Final personal reach out

Hey [Name], one more nudge on the Cancellation Policy signing. This is the last reminder I'll send personally. If it's not done in the next few days, the office team will follow up. It's a standard agreement that every client signs at onboarding and we can't skip it. Let me know if you have any questions about what it covers.

If a Client Asks You About Cancelling

Don't take the cancellation request yourself. Always direct them to the form. Here are short scripts for common situations.

"Can I cancel my subscription?"

If they're past their 6-month minimum: "Sure, the official way to cancel is via the form on our website at physiqueacademy.com/refunds-cancellations. It takes a couple of minutes and you'll get a confirmation email. I can't action cancellations directly."

If they're inside their 6-month minimum: "You're still inside your 6-month minimum so cancellation isn't available yet. You can submit the request via the form when you reach the 6-month mark."

"I want my money back / a refund"

"Refunds aren't part of the cancellation policy. When you cancel, one final payment is still collected as standard - that's what you agreed to in the policy at onboarding. Happy to forward your feedback to the team if there's something specific."

"Just cancel me, here in chat"

"I can't action cancellations through chat - the only valid way is via the form at physiqueacademy.com/refunds-cancellations. It's a quick submission and you'll get a confirmation email. Without it on record we can't process anything."

"I forgot what I signed at onboarding"

"No problem - the policy covers a 6-month minimum, one final payment when you cancel, one month's notice, and cancellation is only through the official form. I can resend you a copy of the signed document if you want."
