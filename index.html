Cancellation Process

How cancellations work at Physique Academy and what each team member is responsible for.

Coaches: there's a shorter guide written specifically for you, focused on the policy basics, how to track your clients' signing status, and message templates for chasing. Open the Coaches Guide.

Why We Have This Process

We need every cancellation request to come through a single official channel with a clear paper trail.

  1. Chargeback / dispute protection. If a client later disputes a payment with Stripe or their bank, we need to prove they read the terms, acknowledged them, and submitted a cancellation request the right way. Without that proof we lose disputes.
  2. Consistency. Clients can't shop around the team trying to find someone who'll give them a different answer. One process, same outcome every time.
  3. Fairness to the client. The form makes clear what they're agreeing to before they submit, so no surprises about the final payment.
All other channels (WhatsApp, email, verbal request to a coach) are not valid and should be politely redirected to the form.

Step 1: Signed Cancellation Policy at Onboarding

Every new client signs a Cancellation & Billing Policy Agreement as part of their onboarding. This is the foundation of our dispute defence. By the time anyone wants to cancel, they've already signed and agreed to the terms.

When it's triggered

Automatically, after the client completes the onboarding questionnaire

Once a new client fills in and submits the long onboarding questionnaire, the cancellation policy document is sent to them via email through GHL Documents & Contracts. No manual step is required from the team.

What the client does

Opens the document, reads, and signs electronically

The policy explains the 6-month minimum, that one final payment will be collected after any cancellation request, and that the official cancellation form is the only valid method.

What happens once signed

The signed document is stored on the client's GHL contact

A URL to the signed contract is saved to the contact's Signed Policy URL field, with a timestamp. This is the first piece of dispute defence evidence we keep on file.

If they haven't signed

Bailey chases via the tracker dashboard

The cancellation tracker dashboard shows everyone who has and hasn't signed. Bailey follows up with anyone outstanding so we don't have clients on the books without a signed agreement.

Ongoing: Automated 7-Day Pre-Payment Notice

This runs in parallel to the subscription, not as a one-off event. Every active client automatically receives a notice email 7 days before each monthly billing for the entire life of their subscription. It's one of Stripe's chargeback-defence requirements and is fully automated. No team action is required.

When it's triggered

Automatically, 7 days before each subscription renewal

Stripe detects the upcoming renewal and sends an event to our system, which then triggers the notice email to the client through GHL.

What the client receives

An email reminding them of the upcoming payment

The email confirms the amount due, the date their card will be charged, and how to contact us if they need to discuss anything. This means no client is ever charged without prior warning.

Step 2: Client Requests Cancellation

When a client wants to cancel, they follow this path:

  1. Visit the cancellation page at physiqueacademy.com/refunds-cancellations (also reachable via /cancel).
  2. Fill in the form: name, email, phone, current duration on program, coach, reason for cancelling, lower-cost option preference, additional comments.
  3. Tick three required acknowledgements:
    • They've completed their 6-month minimum
    • They understand at least one final payment will be collected
    • They understand this form is the only valid cancellation method
  4. Submit.
  5. Receive a confirmation email immediately with their unique cancellation reference (e.g. CXL-AB12CD34). They keep this for their records.
  6. Team member contacts them within 2 business days to confirm their final payment date, end date, and that access continues until then.

Step 3: Who Receives What and What They Do

The Client
Receives Confirmation email immediately with their cancellation reference and the 3 things they acknowledged.
Does Nothing further until a team member follows up within 2 business days.
Bailey (cancellation admin)

Receives

  • Email notification in the Physique Academy GHL inbox
  • Slack alert in #cancellations

Does

  • Within 2 business days, contact the client to confirm their final payment date and end date
  • Process the cancellation in Stripe (set the subscription to cancel at period end)
  • Update the client's Cancellation Status in GHL: Requested → Confirmed → Completed
  • Check the tracker dashboard to chase anyone who hasn't yet signed the cancellation policy
The Selected Coach
Receives In-app GHL notification that their client has requested cancellation.

Does

  • Do not promise refunds or override the policy (one final payment will still be collected)
Management (Jamie Gorse, Neil Chauhan, Sam Parker)

Does

  • Handle escalations (medical, hardship, dispute risk)
  • Review weekly volume and reasons

What We Keep on File as Evidence

For every cancellation request, the following is automatically recorded on the client's GHL contact. This is what we send Stripe if a chargeback dispute is ever raised.

What Clients Should Be Told (Key Talking Points)

  • They must complete their 6-month minimum before they can cancel.
  • They must give at least 1 calendar month's notice, aligned with their payment date.
  • At least one final payment will still be collected after they submit the request. This is not refundable.
  • The only valid way to cancel is via the form at physiqueacademy.com/refunds-cancellations. WhatsApp messages, emails, or verbal requests to a coach do not count.

Common Scenarios

"A client emailed me asking to cancel."

Reply with the form link. Do not action the request via email. Example reply:

To cancel your subscription please complete the official cancellation form: physiqueacademy.com/refunds-cancellations. This is the only way we can process cancellations. You'll get a confirmation email within minutes and a team member will follow up within 2 business days.

"A client is upset about the final payment."

The 3 acknowledgements they ticked on the form make this clear before they submit. Refer them to the cancellation policy they signed at onboarding.

"A client says they cancelled but we still charged them."

Look them up by email in GHL. If they never submitted the form, the cancellation isn't on record and the charge was correct. If they did submit, escalate to Moghees.

"A client wants a refund of the final payment."

The final payment is non-refundable per the terms they agreed to at onboarding and re-confirmed on the cancellation form. Refer them to the policy.
